Project Manifesto

About Study Together

An aesthetic scrapbook virtual co-working space crafted with pure HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript.

The Philosophy: Body Doubling

Psychological research shows that studying in the quiet presence of peers creates gentle social accountability. It reduces procrastination, curbs digital distractions, and helps you enter a deep flow state without the stress of having to talk, present, or turn on your microphone.

Pure & Proper Scrapbook Architecture

  • Pure Semantic HTML5: Accessible, clean structure with zero bulky frameworks or external runtimes.
  • Dual Journal System (Day & Night): Smooth dark/light mode toggle with CSS variables and localStorage persistence.
  • Handcrafted Scrapbook Aesthetics: Washi tape strips, pushpins, polaroids, stamp badges, and lined legal pad to-do lists.
  • Password Visibility: Instant show/hide password toggle button for effortless login.
  • Crisp Vector SVG Icons: Replaced all emojis with sleek, accessible SVG icons that scale cleanly on any screen.
  • Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+): Client-side authentication, URL parameter passing, custom Pomodoro countdown, and task persistence.
  • Zero Backend Friction: Jitsi Meet video co-working embed runs client-side with audio and video safely muted initially.
